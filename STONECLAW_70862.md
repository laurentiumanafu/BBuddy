# 70862 Stoneclaw: explicitly approved proximity heuristic

Place the Stoneclaw Totem spell in a normal first-action-bar slot 1–12 with its normal key binding. If key 1 is reserved for the Start Attack macro, use another slot. Supported cast IDs are 5730, 6390, 6391, 6392, 10427 and 10428. The bot attempts Stoneclaw independently of the self-buff checkboxes; remove it from the bar or set its Spells role to **Ignore** to disable it.

During a fight with an active Grind profile, the bot can attempt the totem if combat is readable and active and it observes at least two distinct living NPC candidates **less than 10 yards** away (3D), each matching any faction ID in the active profile area's `<Factions>` list. The two NPCs do not need to have the same ID. Unreadable, unstable, zero or unlisted faction keys do not count. The filter uses the same experimental faction-key reading as Grind, not a verified reaction or hostility check.

This is a proximity **heuristic**, not attacker detection: a matching friendly, neutral, idle or summoned unit may be counted, while ranged attackers or units outside the client snapshot can be missed. The check excludes player vtables and dead, flagged or unreadable units. Two snapshots must contain the same two matching candidate identities still within range. Action bar, target, player, map, combat, estimated mana, STOP and death/ghost guards are rechecked. A candidate read, key press and totem cast are not proof of a taunt or success. Public data suggests the totem taunts within 8 yards, so a matching NPC between 8 and 10 yards may trigger the attempt without being affected.

If there is no active Grind profile area with a valid faction list, Stoneclaw is not automatically attempted. This includes profile-free Grind and standalone GatherBuddy; the separate faction settings are not substituted for the requested profile list. In a profiled Grind session the same check also applies during defence, including defence while gathering.

Only attempts, not game cooldowns, are tracked: a Stoneclaw key attempt is limited to once per 30 seconds across fights in the session. The rank cost estimates (15, 30, 55, 75, 105 and 140 mana) and 30-second cooldown are estimates from public data, not verified client state. An attempt reserves the Earth slot against automatic Strength of Earth Totem for 20 seconds, even if Stoneclaw failed, to avoid immediately overwriting it. Stoneclaw and Strength of Earth share the Earth slot; Stoneclaw can replace Strength of Earth. After the hold, if combat continues and Strength is absent, the normal combat-buff policy may try to restore it. Manual totem casts, a restart, early destruction, other Earth spells and actual cooldowns are not tracked.

Heal, STOP, death, target identity and fight safety take precedence. Travel does not trigger Stoneclaw; GatherBuddy's Ignore monsters policy is unchanged. Same-element conflicts with other manual or automatic totems are not fully detected. Supervise in-game and report logs if it triggers for non-attackers or fails to trigger.
