# Experimental wowb skinning

This is the ClickAim **Grind** path for builds 70291, 70707, 70755, 70790, 70862, 70888 and 70938. The legacy 3.3.5a bot has its own separate skinning behavior and settings.

1. Train Skinning in-game, bring any required tool, and enable game auto-loot. The bot cannot read the character's profession, skill, tools, loot window or whether a skinning cast succeeded.
2. Stop the bot. Select a **living beast** in WoW. Open **Gather settings** and press **Remember selected beast**, confirming only if it really is a beast. Repeat for each beast type you want to skin.
3. Check **Skin confirmed beasts after looting (Grind)** for your own looted corpses and/or **Skin nearby confirmed dead beasts (Grind)** for nearby bodies regardless of who killed them; each option works independently. Press **Save**. The remembered list is saved immediately; 70291 has its own list and 70707/70755/70790/70862/70888/70938 share the 70707 list. **Clear confirmed beasts** clears both lists.
4. Start Grind. The nearby option searches within 18 yd and approaches on mesh when safe; it is not limited to corpses the character fought and does not first loot them. Combat, pending vendor trips, recent own-corpse looting and STOP take priority. On 70938, nearby confirmed beasts are considered before resource detours and new voluntary pulls, and the closest eligible body is chosen by 3-D distance. Other builds keep their earlier priorities.

On 70938, both post-loot and nearby skinning use a hover-only search: no left-click selection probes on surrounding units. It requires a fresh transition to a twice-read matching candidate mouseover GUID, the same corpse identity/zero-health candidate, range, map, safe combat state, focus and client geometry. Guarded recent loot searches for confirmed beasts also use this hover approach when a skinning option is enabled, rather than selecting nearby totems with left-click probes. Hover and creature entry fields are experimental; if they cannot be verified, interaction is withheld. Other combat or loot routines can still change selection.

A skinning right-click is **not** counted as success. On 70938 a valid, idle cast baseline is required before any click. After a posted click, the bot holds still for the observed ID-10768 cast candidate and a stable clear. Transient unreadable combat, map, player, ghost or cast reads keep it holding, rather than immediately allowing a pull or movement. Vendor requests and skinning setting changes wait until that hold ends. STOP, death and confirmed combat still preempt the wait; a changed attachment or map stops the run. If safe, stable idle cannot be observed within 12 seconds, the bot stops rather than resume movement through a possible cast. The new hold and its safety reasons are logged. If six seconds of readable idle samples show no candidate, it rechecks identity, range and hover and can send one more click. After two such no-cast attempts it gives up on that corpse. It does not retry if a cast was observed, the cast data or a post-click safety read became unknown, a button-up post was uncertain, combat begins or another pre-click safety check fails. This avoids blindly clicking while a cast may be in progress; the experimental candidate still does not prove completed skinning or received items. One right-click may open loot if the corpse isn't ready. The shared bounded per-session attempt cache prevents repeated sessions on the same corpse; a full cache refuses new attempts until the next Grind start. Other builds retain their prior one-click/fallback behavior, including verified F1 for their selection-probe path.

**Limitations:** the unit creature-type and skill offsets are not verified on wowb. Remembering a beast stores an experimental entry candidate extracted from its GUID, not a proven client creature type; the bot treats other units with that candidate as the same beast type. It cannot independently guarantee that matching implies a beast, or that a beast is skinnable, that auto-loot finished, or that items were received. Never confirm an unknown type. A wrong or colliding candidate could cause an unwanted second interaction. Unknown entries are skipped. `[Skin]` logs describe requests and withholding, not confirmed skinning success. Candidate mouseover is also unverified on 70707 and can cause attempts to be withheld. Live validation is required.

## Loot diagnostics on build 70707

Grind revisits recently engaged zero-health candidates, approaches within about 3 yd using the mesh, and rechecks 3D distance and safety before posting the loot interaction. It locates the body with a selection probe, posts a cursor move and gives the client time to process it. The logged mouseover value is only a candidate; it is not an independently verified loot signal. One retry is scheduled only if no right-button-down message was posted. Once a down is sent, the bot does not blindly click again (the separately enabled, confirmed-beast skinning attempt is the exception).

The game must have **auto-loot enabled** for a single right-click to collect items automatically. A posted message, an unchanged or vanished corpse, and the bag-offset research samples do not establish that items were received. If loot still fails, preserve the `[Loot]` lines including the pixel, posted-message reason and candidate hover result, and note whether the game's loot window opened. On build 70707, `[Skin]` may still withhold its second click if the provisional mouseover GUID cannot be validated.
