# WowB death and corpse recovery investigation

**Automatic release, ghost navigation and resurrection are not implemented.** Grind and standalone Gather stop for manual recovery on a readable zero-health candidate. The best-effort checkpoint records the observed position after a zero-health reading only if the same full player GUID and map had a living observation within 30 seconds. On the zero reading, health and identity are sampled twice, and the map and attachment are rechecked. A candidate file is written to `Logs/CorpseRecovery/` at most once per run. It contains the GUID, map and coordinates and may be identifying. Never use this file as proof of a corpse: reads are non-atomic, health zero and position are candidates on 70707, health can become unreadable on death, and a map/identity transition, a fast release, or a bot action blocking the worker may prevent capture or produce a wrong position. Starting while already dead never saves a guessed checkpoint. The file is not consumed by navigation or automatic clicks.

70707 static examination of the decoded PE found the `UnitIsGhost` registration at handler RVA `0x1801D10`: its local-object branch tests byte `unit+0x26D8` bit `0x10` after a type-table check. A fallback branch uses a different pointer and bit. This is only a **static candidate**, not a validated ghost-state decoder. `UnitIsDead` handler RVA `0x17FFCB0` checks health at `+0x1E28` with other flag logic. `RepopMe` and `RetrieveCorpse` handlers exist at `0x1455EC0` and `0x1457A10`, but their internal calls have not been validated; no Lua or internal client functions are invoked.

The stopped-bot Combat research recorder now includes `+0x26D8` in its read-only window (`+0x0000..+0x27FF`). To investigate on 70707, stop the bot and make separate labeled 25-second traces for the **same character**, target cleared: (1) visibly alive, (2) dead with the release prompt still showing, (3) ghost after manually releasing. Do not click a bot control during a recording. If any trace stops early, retain its status; identity or action-bar changes may cause it to stop. These logs include raw memory and GUIDs and may contain sensitive information. A difference in the bit is evidence only, not proof of a safe control or a complete state machine. Screenshot-independent UI visibility and exact release/resurrect control targeting, confirmation transitions, map/identity checks, route reachability and STOP/focus safeguards still need validation before recovery can be enabled. No assumptions are made from health 1 or fixed screen coordinates.
